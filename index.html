    💡 How to Use & Pro Tips

    1. Open this webpage on the devices you want to share files between.
    2. Scan the QR code, or type the 6-digit code of the other device and click Connect.
    3. Select your files (add an optional password if needed) and hit Send!

    ⚠️ Important Rules:

    • The Golden Rule: If one device is using Mobile Data (SIM) and the other is on Wi-Fi, you must enter the Wi-Fi device's code into the Mobile device.
    • Maximum Speed: For lightning-fast transfers, make sure all devices are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.
    • Keep Screen Awake: Do not lock your phone screen or close the browser while a large transfer is running.

    How It Works: The Power of WebRTC

    Instant File Share utilizes modern WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) technology to revolutionize how you transfer data between your personal devices. Unlike traditional cloud storage services (like Google Drive or Dropbox) where you have to upload a file to a remote server and then download it again, our app builds a direct, invisible bridge between your devices.

    When you pair two devices using our 6-digit code or QR scanner, they establish a secure Peer-to-Peer (P2P) connection over your local Wi-Fi network. Your files are chunked into high-speed data packets and streamed directly from one screen to the other.

    Privacy Policy: Your Data Stays Yours

    We believe that your personal files are your business. Because of our strict Peer-to-Peer architecture, we can guarantee total privacy:

    • Zero Server Storage: Your files are never uploaded to, stored on, or passed through our web servers. The transfer happens strictly between your two connected devices.
    • Local Network Speed: Transfers stay on your local Wi-Fi router whenever possible, meaning they don't consume your internet bandwidth.
    • End-to-End Encryption: WebRTC inherently encrypts data in transit. For extra security, you can use our built-in Password Lock feature, which scrambles your file using an XOR cipher before it even leaves your device.
    • No Tracking: We do not require accounts, logins, or personal information. Your connection IDs are randomly generated and discarded the moment you close the browser.
